Requirements & scope

What RetailOS must do, for whom, and in which release. Features have IDs (F-xxx), business rules (BR-xx) and non-functional requirements (NFR-xx), so tickets, tests and code can cite them.

Business goals

IDGoalMeasure of success
BG-1Billing never stops in a shopZero lost sales during internet or cloud outages. Checkout works offline for at least 72 hours.
BG-2Shops comply with Sri Lankan VAT invoicingTax invoices and credit notes accepted by the shop's accountant. Ready for RAMIS e-invoicing within 3 months of the specification being published.
BG-3Accurate stock per batch and expiry dateStock rebuilt from the ledger matches stored balances. Expired-item sales drop to near zero in pilot shops.
BG-4One product for retail, pharmacy and groceryA new industry is enabled per tenant without code changes to the core Sales module.
BG-5Cheap to run and support with a small teamCloud cost per active branch stays under about US$1.50 a month at 100 branches and under US$1 from 300 branches. Most support issues can be diagnosed remotely.

Scope by release

The MVP rule The MVP covers what a cashier needs every day and what a tax auditor will check. Back-office breadth comes later. With one engineer, every MVP item has to earn its place.

MVP In scope

  • Local-first Windows register: offline billing, printing, cash drawer
  • Core catalog with variants, units, multiple barcodes, Excel import
  • Batches, expiry dates, FEFO allocation, GS1 2D barcode parsing
  • Barcodes with weight or price inside them (grocery basics)
  • Discounts, hold/resume, returns and refunds linked to the original receipt
  • Payment types: cash, card (recorded only), LankaQR (recorded only), customer credit
  • Shifts and cash-up, day-end report
  • Tax invoices, credit notes, receipt numbering per register
  • Roles, permissions, staff PINs, audit log
  • English, Sinhala and Tamil
  • Core reports; multi-branch data model
  • Registers on Windows and on Android phones and tablets

Not in MVP

  • Pharmacy and grocery extension modules R2
  • Purchase orders, stock transfers R2
  • Loyalty, SMS/WhatsApp receipts, marketing R2/R3
  • RAMIS e-invoicing API, secured POS adapter R3 (when the IRD publishes it)
  • Integrated card terminals and LankaQR, accounting exports R3
  • AI insights, e-commerce, delivery apps, self-checkout Later
  • Restaurant and salon modules Later

Key user stories

Acceptance criteria use Given/When/Then so they can become automated tests directly.

US-01 · Sell while the internet is down (F-602, F-101)

As a cashier, I want to keep billing when the internet is down, so customers are never turned away.

Given the register has synced its catalog at least once
  And the network cable is unplugged
When I scan 3 items, take cash and press Complete
Then the receipt prints within 2 seconds
  And the receipt number follows this register's sequence with no gap
  And the sale is queued with status PENDING_SYNC
When the network returns
Then the sale reaches the server within 2 minutes
  And it is recorded once, even if it was sent twice
US-02 · Choose the batch when prices differ (F-103)

As a cashier, I want to see which batch I'm selling when batch prices differ, so the customer pays the price printed on the pack.

Given product "Panadol 500mg" has open batches B1 (Rs 30, exp 2027-03) and B2 (Rs 32, exp 2027-09)
When I scan the product without a GS1 2D code
Then a batch picker shows B1 and B2 with price and expiry, B1 preselected (FEFO)
When I scan a GS1 DataMatrix containing batch B2
Then B2 is selected automatically and no picker is shown
Given all open batches have the same price
Then no picker is shown and the server allocates by FEFO
US-03 · Refund against the original receipt (F-107) 
Given receipt CMB01-R02-000154 sold 2 x item A from batch B1
When I scan the receipt barcode and return 1 x item A
Then a return is created linked to the original sale line
  And 1 unit goes back to batch B1
  And a credit note is issued if the original was a tax invoice
  And the refund amount equals the original net line price, never the current price
When the return amount is above the cashier's limit
Then a manager PIN is required and recorded in the audit log
US-04 · Close a shift (F-502) 
Given my shift opened with a float of Rs 5,000
When I close the shift and enter counted cash per denomination
Then the system shows expected cash = float + cash sales - cash refunds + cash in - cash out
  And it records the difference as overage or shortage
  And it prints a shift (Z) report
  And the cashier cannot see the expected cash before counting (blind count, configurable)
US-05 · Receive goods with a GS1 2D code (F-204) 
Given I am receiving a delivery from supplier "Hemas"
When I scan a GS1 DataMatrix with (01)GTIN (17)270930 (10)A123
Then the product is found by GTIN, expiry 2027-09-30 and batch A123 are filled in
  And I only enter the quantity, unit and cost
When the GTIN is unknown
Then I can link it to an existing product or create a new one
US-06 · Credit sale with a limit (F-302) 
Given customer "Perera Stores" has a credit limit of Rs 100,000 and Rs 90,000 outstanding
When I complete a Rs 15,000 sale on credit
Then the register warns that the limit will be exceeded by Rs 5,000
  And it requires manager approval to continue
  And when offline it checks against the last synced balance plus this register's unsynced credit sales
US-07 · Sell a weighed item (F-704) 
Given the scale prints barcode 2 01234 01250 7 (prefix 2, PLU 01234, weight 1.250 kg)
When the cashier scans it
Then the line shows product PLU 01234, quantity 1.250 kg, price = unit price x 1.250
  And the barcode format (weight or price, digit layout) is configurable per tenant
US-08 · Issue a VAT tax invoice (F-1004) 
Given the tenant is VAT-registered
When the customer asks for a tax invoice and gives their VAT number
Then the invoice uses the tax invoice number series, shows the supplier and buyer VAT numbers,
     the date, a description, value excluding VAT, the VAT rate and amount, and the total
  And the invoice can never be edited, only corrected by a credit or debit note

Business rules

IDRule
BR-01Each role has a maximum discount (percentage and amount). A larger discount needs a manager override, which is recorded with who, when and why.
BR-02Price snapshot: a line stores the unit price, discounts, tax and batch cost at the moment of sale. Later price changes never alter past sales (INV-10).
BR-03Rounding: line amounts are calculated to 4 decimal places and rounded to 2 for display and totals using HALF_UP. Optional cash rounding to the nearest Rs 1 is recorded as a separate rounding line.
BR-04Batch selection: if the cashier picked a batch or a GS1 code carried one, that batch is used. Otherwise allocation is FEFO (earliest expiry first), skipping expired and blocked batches (INV-14).
BR-05Short stock never blocks a sale that already happened offline. The server records the sale, lets the balance go negative and raises a NEGATIVE_STOCK exception. Tenants can switch on strict blocking for online registers.
BR-06A return can't exceed the original quantity minus earlier returns. The default return window is 14 days (configurable). Refunds use the original price and payment method unless a manager overrides.
BR-07A credit sale needs a named customer with a credit account. Going over the credit limit needs a manager override.
BR-08A register can't sell without an open shift. One cashier session per register at a time. Opening the drawer without a sale needs permission and is audited.
BR-09Expired batches can't be sold. When the register is offline and its expiry data is stale, it warns instead of blocking, and the server raises EXPIRED_BATCH_SOLD.
BR-10Receipt numbers are {branchCode}-{registerCode}-{sequence}, gap-free per register. Tax invoice numbers use their own gap-free series per register (INV-06).
BR-11Tax rates come from configurable tax rules with valid-from dates and are never hard-coded. Sale lines snapshot the rate applied.

Non-functional requirements

IDQualityRequirement
NFR-01Register speedScan to line on screen in under 100 ms (p95) with 50,000 products in the local catalog.
NFR-02CheckoutComplete to receipt printing starts in under 1.5 s (p95), online or offline.
NFR-03OfflineAt least 72 h of offline selling and at least 10,000 queued sales per register with no data loss.
NFR-04SyncA queued day of sales (about 1,000) uploads within 2 minutes on a 1 Mbps link.
NFR-05AvailabilityBackend 99.5% monthly. Registers don't depend on it for selling.
NFR-06DurabilityRPO ≤ 5 min (point-in-time recovery) and RTO ≤ 4 h. Restore rehearsed every quarter.
NFR-07ScalabilityAt least 1,000 branches and 3,000 registers on one primary database with read replicas. Peak at least 50 sales/s.
NFR-08SecurityOWASP ASVS level 2. No critical or high vulnerabilities open for more than 14 days.
NFR-09IsolationAn automated test in CI proves one tenant can't read another tenant's data.
NFR-10MaintainabilityModule boundaries verified in CI. Core domain test coverage at least 80%.
NFR-11LocalisationEvery UI string in en/si/ta, with no hard-coded text.
NFR-12CompatibilityWindows 10/11 (x64) with 4 GB RAM. Android 10+ phones and tablets with 3 GB RAM; phones with a screen of at least 5.5".
NFR-13UpgradesThe server supports the current and previous register version (N-1). Upgrades don't stop billing.
NFR-14Data retentionSales and tax records kept for the statutory period (to be confirmed with an accountant, at least 6 years by default), then archived.
