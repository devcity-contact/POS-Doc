Plans

Four plans, from a single phone at a small boutique to a chain of branches. Every plan sells offline, keeps the data safe and handles tax correctly; the plans differ in devices, size, depth of stock and reporting, multi-branch features and support.

This is a proposal The commercial model is still open question Q-05. Prices below are a starting point to test with the pilot shops (phase P6) and to fix in P7 (“pricing and contracts”). The plan names, limits and feature split can change; the rules in What every plan gets should not.

What every plan gets

These are never used to make someone upgrade. A shop on the cheapest plan is protected just as well as an enterprise.

  • Offline selling with automatic sync, so a lost connection never stops a sale.
  • Safe data: point-in-time backups, a daily off-site copy, encrypted registers.
  • Security: two-step sign-in for owners, staff PINs, every important action recorded in the audit log. The audit log is recorded on every plan; only the viewer screen is a Business feature.
  • Correct tax: every plan that issues tax invoices gets the legal formats, and the IRD e-invoicing and Secured POS work when it arrives (R3). Compliance is never a paid extra.
  • Your data is yours: a full export of products, customers and sales on any plan, and when leaving (PDPA).
  • English, Sinhala and Tamil in the app and on receipts.

Billing rules

TopicRule
UnitLite is per device. The other plans are per branch per month; each branch gets the plan's included registers.
Trial30 days of Business, free, no card. At the end the owner picks a plan; nothing is deleted.
PayingMonthly or yearly invoice, paid by bank transfer or card through the payment provider's own page. RetailOS never stores card details (INV-11). Automatic card billing can come later.
UpgradeTakes effect at once; the price difference is charged for the rest of the period.
DowngradeTakes effect at the next period. Data is never deleted. If the shop is over a limit (say, 4 users on a 2-user plan), nothing stops working, but nothing new can be added until it is under the limit again.
Late payment14 days of grace with a banner in the admin app. After that the admin app becomes read-only and registers can't open a new shift. A register never stops in the middle of a shift, and offline sales already made always sync.
LeavingA full data export is available for 90 days, then the shop's data is deleted (PDPA).

How the system enforces plans

Plans need a small amount of backend work, added to the build order:

  • Plan catalog in code (BIZ-06): each plan's limits (branches, registers, users, products) and features. tenant.plan already exists in the data model; add-ons use tenant_module, like the industry modules (extensions).
  • Checks on the server: adding a branch, register, user or product checks the limit; a feature outside the plan returns 403 PLAN-001 with what is missing, and the admin app shows an upgrade message instead of an error.
  • Checks on the register: the plan's features reach registers in the settings sync scope, so an offline register knows what to show.
  • Trial and late payment (BIZ-07): trial end date, grace period, read-only mode, and “no new shift” on registers.
  • Changing a plan (SUPP-07): from the support console at first, audited. Self-service plan changes and online payment can come later.
  • Endpoints: GET /api/v1/tenant/plan (plan, limits and current usage) and PUT /api/v1/platform/tenants/{id}/plan.

Do the prices cover the costs?

Hosting costs about $1.5–3.5 per branch per month in the pilot and $0.35–1.3 at a few hundred branches (Cost & scaling), roughly Rs 100–1,050. Even Lite at Rs 990 per device covers hosting once there are more than a handful of shops. The real cost is support time, which is why the cheaper plans lean on the self-service wizard, the import template and in-app help, and the plans with phone support cost more.
