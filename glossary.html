Glossary

One vocabulary for business people, developers and AI agents. Code, tables and UI use these words exactly.

Business terms

TermMeaning
TenantOne customer business (one subscription). It may own many branches.
BranchOne physical shop or outlet of a tenant, with its own stock location and registers.
RegisterA logical till (counter) in a branch, with its own receipt sequence. Code like R02.
DeviceThe physical PC, tablet or phone currently enrolled as a register. It can be replaced while the register stays the same.
Cash session / shiftThe period a cashier works a register, from opening float to close and count.
FloatCash placed in the drawer at the start of a shift, for change.
Blind countThe cashier counts the drawer without seeing the expected amount.
Z-reportThe end-of-shift or end-of-day report of sales, payments and cash variance.
Product / SKU / variantA product is the general item ("Coca-Cola"). A SKU is a specific sellable variant ("Coca-Cola 500 ml"), with its own barcodes and prices.
Unit / conversionThe measure a SKU is counted in (piece, box, kg) and how units relate (1 box = 12 pieces). Stock is stored in the base unit.
Batch (lot)A quantity of a SKU received together, with the same batch number, expiry date and cost.
FEFOFirst Expired, First Out: sell the batch that expires soonest first.
GRN / goods receiptThe record of stock arriving from a supplier. It creates batches.
Price listA named set of prices (retail, wholesale), optionally per branch, with valid-from dates.
Payment type (tender)How a sale is paid: cash, card, LankaQR, customer credit.
Credit sale"Buy now, pay later" for a named customer with a credit account and limit.
Held / parked billAn unfinished sale put aside on a register to serve another customer.
VoidCancelling an open sale or line before payment. After completion, use a return.
Return / refundA new record that reverses some or all lines of a completed sale and pays money back.
ExchangeA return plus a new sale, linked.
Sale exceptionSomething unexpected found when a sale reaches the server (negative stock, price mismatch, expired batch, clock skew). A manager reviews it, and the sale itself stands.
PLUPrice Look-Up code: a short number for loose items (fruit, vegetables), used by scales and cashiers.
Weight-embedded barcodeA barcode printed by a scale that carries the PLU and the weight or price, usually starting with prefix 2.

Tax and compliance

TermMeaning
IRDInland Revenue Department of Sri Lanka.
VATValue Added Tax. Rates are configured as tax rules, never hard-coded.
Tax invoiceAn invoice in the format VAT law prescribes, issued by a VAT-registered business, with its own number series.
Credit note / debit noteDocuments that correct an issued tax invoice down or up. Invoices are never edited.
RAMISThe IRD's Revenue Administration Management Information System. National e-invoicing is submitted through its Web API.
Secured POSPOS machines meeting IRD specifications, required by the VAT (Amendment) Act No. 14 of 2026 from a date still to be prescribed.
Fiscal adapter / FiscalGatewayThe port in the Tax module that sends tax documents to the authority. No-op in the MVP.
NMRANational Medicines Regulatory Authority of Sri Lanka. Relevant to pharmacy rules.
PDPAPersonal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 (Sri Lanka).
PCI-DSSCard industry security standard. RetailOS stays out of its scope by never handling card data.
LankaQRSri Lanka's national QR payment standard.

Technical terms

TermMeaning
Modular monolithOne deployable application divided into modules with enforced boundaries.
Spring ModulithA library that verifies module boundaries and stores domain events reliably.
SPIService Provider Interface: an interface owned by the core that extensions implement.
Local-firstThe app works against its own local database first and syncs with the server in the background.
Outbox (register)A local table of facts waiting to be pushed to the server.
Event publication registry (server)The server-side outbox: events saved with the business change in one transaction and delivered afterwards.
Change feed / cursorThe ordered list of server-side changes (sync_change_log) and the position a register has read up to.
SnapshotA full compressed copy of a scope (e.g. catalog) used to bootstrap or reset a register.
TombstoneA change-feed entry saying a record was deleted.
IdempotencyDoing the same request twice has the same effect as once. Achieved with client UUIDs.
UUIDv7A time-ordered UUID. Created on registers for facts, and good for index locality.
RLSPostgreSQL Row-Level Security: database-enforced filtering of rows by tenant.
Ledger / projectionThe ledger is the append-only list of changes (stock movements). A projection is a derived current state (stock balance) that can be rebuilt from it.
PITRPoint-in-time recovery: restoring the database to any moment using base backups plus WAL logs.
RPO / RTORecovery Point Objective (how much data can be lost) and Recovery Time Objective (how long recovery may take).
Expand/contractA migration pattern: add new schema first (expand), remove old schema only in a later release (contract).
DeviceBridgeThe register port for hardware: print, open drawer, read scanner and scale.
ESC/POSThe command language of most thermal receipt printers.
GS1 / GTIN / AIGS1 sets barcode standards. GTIN is the global product number. Application Identifiers (AIs) like (01), (10) and (17) label the data fields in 2D codes.
DataMatrixA 2D barcode common on medicines. It can carry GTIN, batch, expiry and serial number.
ADRArchitecture Decision Record.
INV-nnA numbered invariant in this documentation. See the list.
