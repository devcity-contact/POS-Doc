Roadmap & phases

Build in vertical slices, put the local register in early, and put real shops on the product before launch. Each phase ends with a test that proves it works, not with a date.

One engineer: be honest about time For one experienced full-stack engineer using AI coding assistants with this documentation as context, the MVP is roughly 10–12 months of focused work, including a pilot. A second engineer from phase 4 cuts this to about 7–8 months and removes the single-person risk. These are planning ranges, so re-estimate after phase 1.
