Data dictionary

Every MVP table explained one by one: what it is for, each column, how it connects to other tables, the rules that protect it, and the screens that use it. The tables and areas are the same as in the whole-database diagram; the exact SQL for the most important tables is in key table definitions.

How to read the relationships “Each sale_line belongs to one sale” means the line has a sale_id column that must be filled in. “One sale has many sale_line rows” is the same link seen from the other side. “May point to” means the column can be empty, for example a sale without a known customer. 1 · Store & staff

Who the business is, where it sells, who works there, which devices are allowed, every shift, and the audit trail. Everything else points back to these tables, so they are created first (migration V1__store_and_staff.sql, roadmap P1).

tenant

One customer business of RetailOS, for example City Pharmacy & Mart. Every other table carries this tenant's id, and row-level security uses it to keep businesses apart.

ColumnTypeMeaningPoints to
tenant_iduuidThe business (on every table, INV-01).
iduuidPrimary key.
nametextLegal business name, printed on tax documents.
vat_numbertextVAT registration number, e.g. 114128890-7000.
statustextACTIVE, TRIAL or SUSPENDED.
plantextSubscription plan, which limits branches and registers.
timezonetextAsia/Colombo; used to work out each sale's business date.

Relationships

  • One tenant has many tenant_module rows (their tenant_id).
  • One tenant has many branch rows (their tenant_id).
  • One tenant has many app_user rows (their tenant_id).

Rules

  • INV-01: every tenant table has tenant_id and a row-level security policy.
  • A suspended tenant's registers keep selling for 14 days, then become read-only.

Used by: A-05, A-80, P-01, P-02
