UI design & wireframes

How RetailOS should look and behave: the design rules, the shared component library, keyboard and touch conventions, and low-fidelity wireframes of the screens that matter most. The full screen list is on Screens & navigation.

Wireframes, not final visuals The wireframes below show layout, content and priority. Colours, logo and final spacing come from the DevCity brand tokens in packages/ui/tokens. Build screens from shared components so a brand change is a token change.

Design principles

#PrincipleIn practice
U1Speed at the counter beats beautyThe scan field is always focused. The most common action is one key. No confirmation dialogs for routine actions. Undo instead of "Are you sure?".
U2Readable at arm's lengthRegister body text at least 16 px. Totals 32–48 px. High contrast. Works in bright shops and on cheap screens.
U3Touch and keyboard, equallyTargets at least 44 × 44 px (56 px for keypads). Every action also has a keyboard shortcut. Scanner input works on any screen.
U4Three languages, no truncationSinhala and Tamil text runs longer and taller than English. Layouts wrap, never clip. Never put text in images (except raster receipts).
U5Always show system stateOnline/offline, items waiting to sync, printer status and training mode are always visible on the register.
U6Errors say what to do next"Printer is out of paper. Load paper and press Reprint", not "Error 0x13". The error code goes in small text for support.
U7Admin is for decisionsDashboards lead with what needs attention and link to the screen that resolves it. Tables export to Excel.
U8Consistent everywhereThe same component, label and icon for the same thing across register and admin (shared packages/ui).

Typography and languages

  • Fonts: Noto Sans for Latin, plus Noto Sans Sinhala and Noto Sans Tamil, bundled with the apps (no CDN, works offline). Tabular numerals for prices and quantities so columns line up.
  • Scale (register): 14 small · 16 body · 18 line name · 24 section · 32 amount due · 48 change due.
  • Scale (admin): 13 small · 14 body · 16 emphasis · 20 page title · 28 KPI.
  • Line height: 1.5 for Sinhala and Tamil (they have taller glyphs). Never fix the height of a text container.
  • Numbers: always Western digits. Currency shown as Rs 1,250.00 (configurable symbol), formatted by Intl.NumberFormat with the en-LK locale, independent of UI language.
  • Product names: show the user's language when filled in, otherwise English. Search matches all three.

Colour and status semantics

Brand paletteRetailOS uses primary #3772FF, success #22C55E, charcoal #272932, off-white #FDFFFC, warning #FF7F11 and error #FF1B1C. The exact light and dark tokens and the contrast-safe text shades are on Google Stitch prompts → token set. The table below describes what each colour means.
MeaningTokenExampleUsed for
Primary action--accentPayOne primary button per screen (Pay, Complete, Save, Post)
Success / online--okSyncedSync done, sale complete, variance zero
Warning / attention--warnOffline · 12 waitingOffline, near expiry, prices may be old, low stock
Danger / blocked--badRecalled batchErrors, blocked batch, void, shortage over threshold
Information--infoRxBadges: prescription, weighed, offer, tax invoice
Neutral--gray-bgHeldStatuses without urgency

Never rely on colour alone. Every status badge has a text label and an icon. Contrast is at least 4.5:1 for text (WCAG 2.2 AA). Both light and dark themes are supported, and the register defaults to light for bright shops.

Screen states

Every screen and data component must design all of these, not only the "happy" state:

StateRegisterAdmin
LoadingRare (local data). Skeleton for slow lookups over 200 ms.Skeleton rows/tiles. Never a blank page.
Empty"Scan an item to start", with quick keys visibleAn explanation and the next action ("Import products", "Add supplier")
OfflineYellow banner with waiting count. Everything works.Full-width notice. Forms keep their values. Retry when back online.
ErrorInline message + what to do + code. Sale data is never lost.ErrorPanel with retry. Field errors next to fields.
Permission deniedOpens the manager override (R-08)Hidden in navigation. A direct URL shows "You don't have access. Ask the owner."
ConflictNot applicable (facts can't conflict)"Changed by Nimal at 10:42. Reload to see changes."

Accessibility

  • WCAG 2.2 AA target for the admin web. The register meets contrast, focus and target-size rules.
  • A visible focus ring on everything, and a logical tab order. Modals trap focus and return it on close.
  • Radix primitives give correct ARIA roles. Icons have labels. Charts have a data-table alternative.
  • Sound cues on the register (scan OK, scan error, sale complete), plus a visual cue. Volume can be adjusted.
  • Respect reduced-motion settings. Use no animations longer than 200 ms on the register.

UI checklist for every screen

  • Screen ID in the file header and the page title (for support and search).
  • All strings are i18n keys. Checked in si and ta at the smallest supported size.
  • Loading, empty, error, offline and permission states designed and tested.
  • Keyboard reachable. Shortcut listed in the F1 sheet (register).
  • Money uses Money/MoneyField, and dates use branch time zone formatting.
  • Built only from packages/ui components. A new component needs a catalog page.
  • Playwright test for the main path (register screens: also offline).
